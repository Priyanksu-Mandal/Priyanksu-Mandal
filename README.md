<p>
  I’m an <b>Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)</b> undergraduate at <b>IIIT-Delhi</b> with a strong passion for hardware design, microcontrollers, and firmware architecture. 
  I love working at the physical layer: crafting custom circuit schematics, designing real-time sensor monitoring units, and optimizing firmware on <b>STM32 (ARM Cortex-M)</b> and <b>ESP</b> platforms.
</p>

<p>
  Currently, I am conducting research and developing smart embedded systems at the <b>CPID Lab, IIIT-Delhi</b> under the mentorship of <b>Prof. Anuj Grover</b>.
</p>
