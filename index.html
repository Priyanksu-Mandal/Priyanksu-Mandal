About Me

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering undergraduate at IIIT-Delhi (2023–2027). My core technical domain lies at the intersection of physical hardware, silicon architectures, and low-level firmware.

Currently, I conduct research and development under the guidance of Prof. Anuj Grover at the CPID Lab (Circuits, Processing, & Integrated Devices), focusing on smart robotic automation, sensor telemetry, and RTOS firmware.

Outside engineering, I am a trained Indian Classical Sitar player holding a 6th-Year Senior Diploma from Prayag Sangeet Samiti, an All-India CCRT National Scholarship recipient, and a competitive badminton player.
